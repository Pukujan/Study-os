# Build Gates

Study OS uses evidence gates to prevent technically attractive features from outrunning the research question.

## R0 — One auditable learning trajectory

Required before broad product/UI work:

- one complete Subject 001 / Sliding Window trajectory;
- private raw evidence hashed;
- transcript normalized with provenance;
- failure transition recorded as hypothesis;
- intervention representation + operation versioned;
- immediate behavioral outcome measured;
- assistance faded;
- transfer item attempted;
- delayed retrieval attempted;
- self-report compared to behavior;
- experiment reconstructable by a new agent from repo artifacts.

Allowed work before R0:

- research/docs;
- schemas;
- ingest and normalization;
- deterministic DSA state model;
- small representation renderers;
- evaluation harness;
- hidden fixtures;
- CI/security/data-integrity controls;
- agent handoff;
- optional FOSSIL export experiments.

Deferred before R0:

- production web application;
- continuous deployment;
- video-generation pipeline;
- generated-image learning pipeline;
- generalized recommendation model;
- multiple DSA families at scale.

## R1 — Repeated within-subject evidence

Required before claiming a reliable personalized procedure for Subject 001:

- intervention effects replicated across multiple matched problems/sessions;
- at least one counterexample where preferred representation did not improve outcome;
- at least one detected/corrected learner-state misdiagnosis;
- evidence of scaffold fading rather than growing dependency;
- retention checks across multiple concept instances;
- research overhead remains acceptable to the learner.

## R2 — Procedure tested with another participant

Required before making broader learner claims:

- informed data/privacy boundary for additional participant;
- same canonical event/episode schemas;
- attempt to replicate the **procedure**, not Subject 001's representation preferences;
- report differences and failures rather than tuning away every mismatch;
- no population-level efficacy claim from two people.

## Later gates

Future gates may cover:

- generalized learner-policy evaluation;
- production privacy/security;
- multi-domain expansion;
- deployable application UX;
- FOSSIL-backed long-lived knowledge synthesis;
- controlled studies with larger groups.

Define those only when current evidence justifies them.
